# SHP REMOTE — HARDWARE & WIRING GUIDE

## 1. Hardware Requirements

- **ESP32 DevKit V1 (ESP-WROOM-32)**
- **USB Cable:** Standard Micro-USB / USB-C data cable plugged into your Windows 11 laptop.
- **Power:** 5V USB bus (240mA peak during Wi-Fi transmission).
- **Optional Status LED:**
  - GPIO 2: Built-in Blue LED (SoftAP indicator)
  - GPIO 4: Optional Green LED with 220Ω series resistor (Companion connection active)

## 2. Pinout & Antenna Care

- Keep the top antenna portion clear of metal enclosures.
- USB port provides both 5V power and 115200 baud serial monitoring output.
